What we do

Six services. One ready-to-live home.

From the moment the last box is dropped off to the moment you put your feet up — every step of turning a house into a home, handled by trained, verified teams.

01

Packers & Movers

Safe packing, careful loading, transport and unloading — handled by trained, verified teams who treat your belongings like their own, from your old door to your new one.

02

Unpacking

Careful unpacking and item management so boxes don't sit around for weeks. Everything is sorted, checked against your inventory and placed where it belongs.

03

Home Cleaning

A deep clean of your new space before anything is placed — floors, kitchen, bathrooms and every corner, so day one actually feels like a fresh start.

04

Furniture Setup

Dismantling, re-assembly and proper placement of beds, wardrobes, sofas and shelving — done right the first time, with tools and hardware handled for you.

05

Home Organisation

Smart, livable organisation for every room, cupboard and shelf — arranged around how you'll actually use your kitchen, wardrobe and storage, not just where boxes landed.

06

Appliance & Ready-to-Live Setup

Installation and basic function checks for your TV, AC, washing machine and other appliances — plus a complete home setup so you can move in and simply relax.
