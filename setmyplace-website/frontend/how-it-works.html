The process

One call. One booking. Zero stress.

Whether you're booking a home setup or referring us your customers as a partner, here's exactly what happens at every step.

For customers

Booking your ready-to-live setup.

From the first call to putting your feet up in an organised home — four simple steps.

1

Contact us

Call or WhatsApp your requirement — tell us your move-in date and what your new home needs.

2

Get a quote

Receive a clear, itemised quote for exactly the services you want, with no hidden costs.

3

Book & relax

Confirm your booking on a date that suits you — we schedule around your move.

4

Move. Set. Live.

Our trained team handles everything on-site, while you enjoy the new home.

For partners

Referring a customer as a partner.

Keep doing what you do best — moving belongings — and let us handle the home setup that comes after.

1

You get a moving order

You do the packing, loading, transportation and unloading, as usual.

2

Customer needs post-move setup

After moving, the customer needs unpacking, cleaning, setup or full home organisation.

3

Share the lead with us

Share customer details or connect them directly with Set My Place.

4

We coordinate & execute

Our team takes it from there — unpacking, cleaning, setup and home organisation.

5

Customer gets a ready-to-live home

Your customer enjoys a hassle-free, well-organised new home — and remembers you for it.

6

You earn your partner benefit

You earn your agreed referral or partner commission — for work you didn't have to do.
